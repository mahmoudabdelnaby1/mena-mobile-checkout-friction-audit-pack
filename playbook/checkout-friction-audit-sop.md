# MENA Mobile Checkout Friction Audit SOP (Salla / Zid / Shopify)
1. **Address Form Simplification:** Replace multi-field city/district/street inputs with auto-complete National Address or single Google Maps geopin.
2. **Payment Method Prioritization:** Surface Apple Pay and Mada at the top for GCC; surface Fawry and Instapay for Egypt.
3. **Transparent Surcharges:** Expose COD handling fees on the product page rather than at the final payment step.
